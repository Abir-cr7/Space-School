Why Does NASA Leave Hardware on Other Worlds?

Three vital scientific and engineering reasons

📡

Long-Term Science Experiments

Instruments like the Apollo 11 Laser Retroreflectors and InSight seismometers continued collecting science data for years after launch.

⚖️

Rocket Fuel & Physics

Lifting heavy descent stages and rovers off planetary surfaces requires immense fuel. Leaving them behind allows lightweight return capsules.

🏛️

Planetary Heritage Sites

These historic sites are preserved forever in space as eternal monuments to human curiosity, peaceful exploration, and technological ingenuity.
